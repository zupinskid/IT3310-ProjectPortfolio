## Professional introduction

Welcome to my IT 3310 project portfolio. In this course, I serve as an Associate Consultant at NovaTech Consulting. I will use this repository to organize project management artifacts, explain decisions, and document my growth throughout one continuing client engagement.

## NovaTech and the RCHN engagement

NovaTech Consulting is the consulting organization used in our semester-long course scenario. Its client, Riverside Community Health Network (RCHN), is a nonprofit health network operating six community clinics across the Chicago metropolitan area. RCHN wants to replace its aging patient intake and scheduling system because the current process is creating problems for staff and patients.

The engagement begins before a formal project charter, detailed requirements, or delivery plan has been approved. My portfolio will show how the work develops from onboarding and initiation through planning, delivery coordination, monitoring, and closure. As an Associate Consultant, I will document business needs, distinguish facts from assumptions, communicate clearly, and connect project management concepts to the client's situation.
